Our Mission

We're here to make a dent in the mental health crisis.

Mental health in America is deteriorating. The numbers aren't abstract - they're people. Our families, our friends, ourselves. We believe the supplement industry has a role to play, and most of it is failing that responsibility.

Our founder knows this firsthand. At 17, Kyle drove an ATV off a cliff at 65 mph. Three brain bleeds. Nine days in the hospital. Years of therapy. But the physical injuries were only half of it. The mental health fallout was worse. Depression. Isolation. No desire to be around people. A version of himself he didn't recognize.

His testosterone came back at sub-200 ng/dL - lower than the average 80-year-old. The healthcare system had nothing for him. No real answers, no real solutions. So he started figuring it out himself - obsessively researching every ingredient, every mechanism, every dosage. Testing everything on his own body for over a decade.

He went from sub-200 to over 800 ng/dL. Without TRT. The depression lifted. The fog cleared. He got his life back - not through the system, but in spite of it.

That's why Formulation Factory exists. Not to sell capsules. To give people the tools to take control of their own biology when the system won't do it for them. Every ingredient published. Every dose transparent. Every batch tested. Because the person on the other end deserves better than what this industry has been giving them.

The supplement industry has a trust problem.

Proprietary blends that hide the fact that key ingredients are underdosed. Brands that claim "third-party tested" but have never published a single COA. Influencers who promote products they've never taken for a check they'll never disclose. Labels designed to look impressive while delivering almost nothing.

Most supplement companies are marketing operations that happen to sell capsules. The product is an afterthought. The formulation is whatever's cheapest. The consumer is treated like someone who won't read the label.

We think that's ending. Consumers are getting smarter. They're reading labels. They're asking for proof. And the brands that can't provide it are going to disappear. We exist because we believe the supplement industry should be held to the same standard as any other industry that puts something in your body. Full transparency. Published testing. Real doses. Real science.

The platforms have automated most of the tactical media buying work. Advantage+, PMax, and Smart+ campaigns handle the bid optimization that used to take 30 hours a week. That's why this role exists as a hybrid. You own paid media strategy and execution across all three channels. You also own CRO strategy for the Shopify storefront - deciding which landing pages to build, which A/B tests to run, and how offer architecture impacts conversion. The actual Shopify build work gets executed by an offshore CRO firm that you manage and hold accountable.

The feedback loop between "what ad is winning" and "what landing page should match it" lives in one brain - yours. When a creative angle takes off, you brief the CRO team on the matching LP within hours, not days. When a landing page underperforms, you know whether the problem is the page or the traffic. You think in funnels, not just ad accounts.

Mission

Own Formulation Factory's content distribution infrastructure and social media presence - building a network of accounts, clip channels, and distribution systems that multiply every piece of content we create into maximum impressions - so every video, post, and asset reaches 10x the audience it would on brand accounts alone.

Role Context

Most supplement brands post content to their own accounts and call it a day. We're building something different. We want a distribution machine - a network of clip channels, repost accounts, fan pages, and syndication systems across every platform that turns one piece of content into dozens of touchpoints.

You understand the world of clipping at a deep level. You know how clip channels on YouTube, TikTok, and X built massive audiences by repurposing creator content. You've seen how livestreamers on Twitch and Kick use clip distribution to grow their reach exponentially. Now you're going to apply that playbook to a brand.

You'll manage the Formulation Factory brand accounts across all platforms while simultaneously building and managing a network of distribution accounts - clip channels, topic-specific pages, niche repost accounts - that extend our reach far beyond what a single brand account can achieve. You'll build the system so others clip and distribute for us at scale.

You'll work closely with the Director of Social Commerce on UGC army efforts, feed heavily into the creative pipeline, and develop detailed, calculated distribution systems - not vibes, not guesswork - real systems with tracking, attribution, and measurable impression multipliers.
