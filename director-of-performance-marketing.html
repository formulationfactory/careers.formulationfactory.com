Director of Performance Marketing

Last updated: May 2026

VP of eCommerce

Remote (U.S. only)

$130K-$160K base + performance bonus

Mission

Own the full performance funnel at Formulation Factory - paid media strategy and execution across Meta, TikTok, and Google, plus CRO strategy for the Shopify storefront - so every ad dollar has a matching, optimized conversion experience on the other end, and DTC scales profitably.

Role Context

The platforms have automated most of the tactical media buying work. Advantage+, PMax, and Smart+ campaigns handle the bid optimization that used to take 30 hours a week. What hasn't been automated is the thinking - which creative angles to test, which offers to scale, how to match ad messaging to landing page experience, and when to shift budget between channels.

That's why this role exists as a hybrid. You own paid media strategy and execution across all three channels. You also own CRO strategy for the Shopify storefront - deciding which landing pages to build, which A/B tests to run, and how offer architecture impacts conversion. The actual Shopify build work gets executed by an offshore CRO firm that you manage and hold accountable.

The feedback loop between "what ad is winning" and "what landing page should match it" lives in one brain - yours. When a creative angle takes off, you brief the CRO team on the matching LP within hours, not days. When a landing page underperforms, you know whether the problem is the page or the traffic. You think in funnels, not just ad accounts.

Scorecard - Outcomes

This is how we measure success. Not effort, not hours - outcomes with metrics and deadlines.

By Day 30
  • Full paid media audit complete: reviewed all active ad accounts across Meta, TikTok, and Google. Documented current spend, ROAS/MER, creative performance, campaign structure, audience strategy, and attribution setup. Identified what's working, what's wasted, and the top 5 scaling opportunities.
  • Shopify CRO audit complete: reviewed every key page - PDPs, landing pages, cart, checkout, post-purchase. Documented current conversion rate, page speed, mobile experience, and UX issues. Prioritized fix list.
  • Agency and vendor assessment: evaluated all existing paid media and CRO vendors/agencies. Recommendation to VP of eCommerce: keep, replace, bring in-house, or outsource.
  • CRO execution partner identified: if not already in place, sourced and vetted an offshore CRO firm. Evaluated portfolio, turnaround speed, Shopify depth, and communication quality. Onboarding started or recommendation delivered.
  • Funnel strategy defined: mapped the current state of every active funnel (ad > LP > purchase). Identified gaps, mismatches between ad messaging and landing experience, and the first 3 funnels to build or optimize.
By Day 60
  • Paid campaigns restructured or optimized: campaign structure cleaned up across all channels. Budget reallocation based on audit findings. Creative testing methodology documented. Quick-win optimizations shipped.
  • CRO firm onboarded and executing: offshore team is building landing pages and running A/B tests from your briefs. First deliverables reviewed and live. Communication cadence and QA process established.
  • First CRO improvements live: hero PDPs optimized, cart experience improved, trust elements added. At least 2-3 landing pages rebuilt or newly built and running with paid traffic. Measurable CVR improvement vs. baseline.
  • A/B testing program running: at least 2-3 tests live (headlines, layouts, CTAs, pricing presentation, offer framing). Results logged, learnings applied. Testing methodology documented.
  • Weekly performance dashboard live: covering ad spend by channel, ROAS/MER, CVR by funnel, AOV, revenue per session, and CRO test results. Reviewed in operating meeting.
By Day 90
  • MER improving or stable as spend scales: paid media is running more efficiently than at hire. Spend can be increased without MER degrading.
  • 3+ ready-to-scale funnels live: each with a tested ad angle, matching landing page, and documented playbook. At any point you can increase spend 2-3x on these funnels without conversion rate dropping.
  • CRO firm producing consistently: turnaround on new landing pages is 2-4 business days from brief. Quality is high. You're managing output, not micromanaging execution.
  • Creative feedback loop running: you are briefing the creative team on what angles, hooks, and formats are performing in ads. Data from paid is informing content production weekly.
  • Landing page machine operational: when a new ad angle wins, the matching LP is briefed same day and live within 48-72 hours via the CRO firm. No bottleneck between winning creative and optimized landing experience.
By Month 6
  • DTC revenue grown significantly: DTC as a percentage of total revenue is meaningfully higher than at hire, with profitable unit economics.
  • MER at or above target: blended ROAS across all paid channels is improving or stable at higher spend levels.
  • CVR improved 20%+ vs. baseline: through cumulative CRO improvements, landing page optimization, and offer testing. Documented with data.
  • AOV improved 15%+ vs. baseline: through bundles, upsells, and offer architecture optimized through testing.
  • 5+ high-performing funnels live: each documented with playbooks so the team can scale spend without you personally managing every campaign.
  • Offshore CRO team is a competitive advantage: faster execution at lower cost than an in-house Shopify dev. The relationship you manage produces quality work at speed.
Ongoing KPIs

Once the system is built, these are the numbers you own and review weekly. This is your scoreboard.

  • MER / blended ROAS: total DTC revenue divided by total ad spend. The primary efficiency metric.
  • DTC revenue: total Shopify revenue. Tracked daily, reported weekly.
  • Ad spend by channel: Meta, TikTok, Google. Budget allocation reviewed weekly.
  • ROAS by channel: per-channel return on ad spend. Informing budget shifts.
  • Site-wide conversion rate: tracked daily, improving through continuous CRO.
  • CVR by landing page/funnel: each page tracked individually. Underperformers fixed or killed.
  • AOV: influenced through bundles, upsells, and offer architecture.
  • Revenue per session: CVR x AOV. The single best metric for storefront performance.
  • A/B tests running: number of active tests. Target: 2-3 minimum always running.
  • LP turnaround time: time from brief to live via CRO firm. Target: 2-4 business days.
  • Ready-to-scale funnels: number of tested, profitable funnels. Target: 3+ at all times.

Explicit Boundaries

This role does not own:

  • Organic social or content distribution - that belongs to the distribution function
  • Email/SMS (Klaviyo) - that's a separate function
  • Amazon PPC - that's managed within the Amazon function
  • Product vision, formulation philosophy, or brand worldview - those belong to the Founder

You own every paid dollar and every conversion on DTC. From ad spend allocation to landing page performance. The ad gets them to the site. The page converts them. You own both sides of that equation.

Decision Rights

Owns: Paid media strategy and execution (Meta, TikTok, Google), CRO strategy, A/B testing program, landing page briefing and QA, offer and bundle architecture, offshore CRO team management, ad creative briefing and performance analysis, and weekly performance reporting.

Reports to: VP of eCommerce.

Collaborates with: Creative team on ad production and creative testing, Director of Distribution on organic amplification of winning content, operations on fulfillment capacity, and CX on post-purchase experience.

This Role Is Not
  • A pure media buyer who manages ad accounts but has no opinion on what happens after the click
  • A CRO specialist who optimizes pages but doesn't understand where the traffic comes from
  • A generalist marketer with paid media as one of five responsibilities
  • Someone who needs an in-house Shopify developer to execute every landing page change

This is a performance marketer who owns the full funnel - from ad spend to conversion. You decide what ads to run and what landing pages to build. You manage the team that builds them. You own the number.

Ideal Candidate
  • 4-7 years in performance marketing / paid media with hands-on experience across Meta, TikTok, and Google at a DTC brand doing $5M-$30M+
  • Has personally managed $50K+/month in ad spend and scaled campaigns profitably
  • CRO fluency - you understand what makes landing pages convert and can evaluate Shopify work quality even if you're not the one building it
  • Experience managing offshore teams, agencies, or contractors - you know how to brief, QA, and hold external partners accountable to quality and speed
  • Thinks in funnels - you've built the ad-to-LP-to-purchase pipeline, not just managed one piece of it
  • Strong creative instincts - you can brief creative teams on hooks, angles, and formats that perform, backed by data from your campaigns
  • Comfortable with A/B testing methodology and tools (Convert, VWO, Optimizely, or Shopify native)
  • Supplement, health, wellness, or fitness brand experience strongly preferred
  • Proficient with AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) for workflow acceleration
  • U.S.-based - this is a U.S.-only hire

Bonus: You've built the full funnel at a brand - owned paid, owned CRO, managed the execution team - and have the MER and CVR data to prove it worked.
